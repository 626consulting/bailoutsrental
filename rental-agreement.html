Customer responsibilities

1. Confirmation and rental period

A request does not reserve equipment. The rental begins and ends at the date and time written above or in a written confirmation accepted by both parties. Extensions require BAIL OUT approval in advance; any added charge must be disclosed and accepted before the extension. Equipment is not released until required payment and identification are completed.

2. Site access and placement

The customer must provide a safe, legal, accessible location and obtain any required property permission or permit. Tell BAIL OUT about soft ground, buried utilities, septic systems, overhead lines, gates, slopes, and other site hazards before delivery. BAIL OUT may refuse an unsafe placement. A $100 failed dumpster delivery fee applies if the trip cannot be completed because access or the site is not ready, as disclosed before the rental. BAIL OUT remains responsible for its own negligent acts.

3. Dumpster loading and materials

Do not move, alter, overfill, burn in, or place weight above the rim of the dumpster. Keep the load level and safe for pickup. No hazardous waste, fuel, chemicals, wet paint, propane tanks, batteries, asbestos-containing material, or other material the disposal facility cannot accept. Ask before loading tires, appliances, electronics, concrete, dirt, shingles, or other heavy or special material. The customer is responsible for documented disposal, removal, or return-trip costs caused by an unapproved load.

4. Trailer towing and use

Only an authorized licensed driver may tow the trailer. Before pickup, the renter must show proof of insurance and use a legally registered tow vehicle with adequate towing capacity and the correct hitch, lights, safety chains, and brakes when required. The renter must inspect the connection, observe the trailer's stated load rating, and secure the load. No subletting, unauthorized drivers, alteration, illegal use, or use beyond the agreed purpose without written approval.

5. Condition and damage

BAIL OUT and the customer should record the equipment's condition with photos or written notes at release and return. Report any existing damage before use, and report any accident, theft, missing part, or new damage promptly. The customer is responsible for reasonable documented repair or replacement costs for loss or damage beyond ordinary wear that the customer, an authorized user, or an invited person causes through misuse, negligence, or breach of this agreement. The customer is not responsible for defects or damage caused by BAIL OUT.

6. Pickup and additional charges

Provide clear access for pickup or return the trailer by the agreed time. BAIL OUT will itemize any additional charges and provide supporting information on request. Charges for extra days, unsuccessful pickup trips, special materials, excessive cleaning, missing components, or trailer late return apply only when the amount or method was disclosed in the confirmed quote or separately approved before the charge, except reasonable documented repair or cleanup costs under this agreement.

7. Property and third party claims

The customer accepts the ordinary risk that heavy equipment may mark a customer-selected surface and must disclose known site conditions. Each party remains responsible for loss to the extent caused by its own conduct. The customer will reimburse BAIL OUT for reasonable third party claims to the extent caused by the customer's negligent or unauthorized use, unsafe loading, or breach of this agreement. This does not require the customer to cover BAIL OUT's negligence, equipment defects, or conduct for which BAIL OUT is legally responsible.

8. Other terms

BAIL OUT may delay service for unsafe weather, road, site, or equipment conditions and will communicate changes. The confirmed quote controls the price and approved dates; any separate written cancellation terms must be given before payment. Iowa law governs this agreement. If one provision cannot be enforced, the remaining terms continue to the extent permitted by law. Electronic signatures may be used when both parties agree to transact electronically.

Review and sign

By signing, the customer confirms receipt of these terms, the stated rental details, and the opportunity to inspect the equipment. A signature does not waive rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.
