Home Cleanout Dumpster Questions

What size dumpster is best for a home cleanout? +

A 15-yard dumpster can work well for smaller household and garage cleanouts. The 20-yard dumpster offers more capacity for larger home and property cleanouts.

How much is the 15-yard dumpster? +

The 15-yard dumpster is $375 for 7 days or $475 for 14 days. Two tons of disposal are included. Additional weight is $100 per ton.

How much is the 20-yard dumpster? +

The 20-yard dumpster is $450 for 7 days or $550 for 14 days. Two tons of disposal are included. Additional weight is $100 per ton.

Is standard dumpster delivery included? +

There is no separate charge for standard dumpster delivery. Your delivery address and requested rental dates still need to be confirmed.

Can I use a dumpster while moving or downsizing? +

Yes. Roll-off dumpsters can be useful for removing unwanted household items while moving, downsizing or cleaning a property.

Can everything from my home go in the dumpster? +

Some materials may be restricted or require special handling. Contact BAIL OUT before loading anything you are unsure about.
