Frequently Asked Questions

Construction Dumpster Rental Questions

What size dumpster should I use for a construction project? +

BAIL OUT offers both 15-yard and 20-yard roll-off dumpsters. The 15-yard option works well for smaller remodeling and cleanup projects, while the 20-yard dumpster provides additional capacity for larger renovation and construction projects.

How much does a 15-yard dumpster cost? +

The 15-yard dumpster is $375 for 7 days or $475 for 14 days. Two tons of disposal are included. Additional weight is $100 per ton.

How much does a 20-yard dumpster cost? +

The 20-yard dumpster is $450 for 7 days or $550 for 14 days. Two tons of disposal are included. Additional weight is $100 per ton.

Is standard dumpster delivery included? +

Standard dumpster delivery does not have a separate delivery charge. Availability and placement details are confirmed before the rental.

Do you work with contractors and property managers? +

Yes. BAIL OUT provides roll-off dumpster rentals for contractors, property managers, homeowners and businesses working on construction, remodeling and property projects.

Are there materials I should not put in the dumpster? +

Some materials may be restricted or require special handling. Contact BAIL OUT before loading anything you are unsure about so we can confirm whether it is allowed.

How do I request a construction dumpster? +

You can request a rental online through bailoutrentals.com or call BAIL OUT at 641-691-8799 to check availability.
