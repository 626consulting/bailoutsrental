Frequently Asked Questions

Dumpster Rental Questions

Get answers to some of the most common rental questions.

What dumpster sizes are available? +

BAIL OUT offers both 15-yard and 20-yard roll-off dumpsters. All rentals are subject to availability and confirmation.

How much is a 15-yard dumpster? +

The 15-yard dumpster is $375 for 7 days or $475 for 14 days. The rental includes 2 tons of disposal. Additional disposal weight is $100 per ton.

How much is a 20-yard dumpster? +

The 20-yard dumpster is $450 for 7 days or $550 for 14 days. The rental includes 2 tons of disposal. Additional disposal weight is $100 per ton.

Do you charge for dumpster delivery? +

Standard dumpster delivery has no separate delivery charge. Submit your service address so BAIL OUT can confirm availability and placement details. A $100 failed-delivery fee may apply if the site is blocked, unsafe or inaccessible at the scheduled time.

Which dumpster size should I choose? +

The 15-yard dumpster is a good option for smaller cleanouts, garage projects, moving and smaller renovations. The 20-yard dumpster provides more room for larger cleanouts, construction and renovation projects. If you're unsure, call 641-691-8799 and tell us about your project.

Can I request a dumpster online? +

Yes. Choose the 15-yard or 20-yard dumpster above and submit the rental request, or use the general quote form below.
