Frequently Asked Questions

Dumpster Rental Questions

What dumpster sizes are available? +

BAIL OUT offers both 15-yard and 20-yard roll-off dumpsters.

How much is a 15-yard dumpster? +

$375 for 7 days or $475 for 14 days. Two tons of disposal are included.

How much is a 20-yard dumpster? +

$450 for 7 days or $550 for 14 days. Two tons of disposal are included.

Do you charge for dumpster delivery? +

Standard dumpster delivery has no separate delivery charge. Submit your service address so BAIL OUT can confirm availability and placement details.

Which dumpster size should I choose? +

The 15-yard works well for smaller cleanouts and remodeling projects. The 20-yard provides additional capacity for larger cleanouts, renovation and construction projects.

Can I request a rental online? +

Yes. Choose your rental above or complete the rental request form below.
