Frequently Asked Questions

Rental Questions

Have questions before submitting your request? Here are a few of the basics.

What dumpster sizes are available?

BAIL OUT offers both 15-yard and 20-yard roll-off dumpsters. The 20-yard dumpster is now available. All bookings remain subject to confirmation.

How long can I keep my rental?

Dumpster rentals are available for 7-day or 14-day rental periods. The 15-yard dumpster is $375 for 7 days or $475 for 14 days. The 20-yard dumpster is $450 for 7 days or $550 for 14 days. Both include 2 tons of disposal. Trailer rental periods may vary.

Do you deliver dumpsters?

Delivery is available. Submit your delivery address with your rental request so BAIL OUT can confirm the details for your location.

What trailers are available?

Trailer availability may vary. Use the trailer request form to tell us what you need to haul and when you need it so the appropriate rental option can be confirmed.

Can I request a rental online?

Yes. Select one of the rental options above to submit a request for that specific item, or use the general quote form below if you aren't sure which rental is right for you.
